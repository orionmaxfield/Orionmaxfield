If you need help now

These aren't mine, and they're better than anything I could write for a night that's going badly.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — call or text 988, any time, anywhere in the US.
Crisis Text Line — text HOME to 741741.
Findahelpline.com — for numbers outside the US.

If you're looking for a therapist, Psychology Today's directory and Open Path Collective (for lower-cost sessions) are the two places I'd start.

This site is writing, not therapy. Reading it doesn't create a counseling relationship, and nothing here is a substitute for care from a licensed professional who knows your situation.

If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US).
